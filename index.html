📜 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how AARA Apps ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Vetri Tamil Calendar app for Android and iOS ("the App").

Vetri Tamil Calendar is a Tamil calendar and devotional app. It offers Tamil calendar and panchangam data, Nalla Neram and daily timings, festivals and Tamil Nadu Government holidays with reminders, devotional lyrics, God stories, gold & silver rates, home-screen widgets where your device supports them, and optional paid features.

Android app only: the Android app also includes astrology features: Rasi Palan, Jathagam (birth chart) profiles and calculations, and Porutham matching, with optional extra Jathagam profile slots. These features are not part of the iOS app. Wherever this policy mentions Jathagam, Rasi Palan, Porutham or Jathagam slots, it applies to the Android app only.
Plain-language summary: The App has no account or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name or email. On Android, birth details you enter for Jathagam profiles stay on your device. Like most free apps, the App uses Google's advertising, analytics and crash-reporting tools, which collect device identifiers and approximate location. We do not sell your personal data. Ads can be removed with a paid plan.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and stop using it.

📱 Data Kept on Your Device

The following stay on your device and are not sent to us or to our servers:

  • Jathagam and family profiles Android only: the names, birth dates, birth times and birth places you enter, and the charts calculated from them.
  • Bookmarks, favourites, reminders and notification preferences.
  • Your chosen city and app settings.

If Android backup or iCloud Backup is turned on for your device, your device may include the App's local data in that backup. That copy is held by Google or Apple under your account, not by us. You can delete a profile inside the App at any time, or turn off backup in your device settings.

📍 Location

The App may ask for your location so that it can use your nearest city for these features:

  • Sunrise and sunset times
  • Panchangam (Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana) calculations
  • Rahu Kalam and Yamagandam timings
  • Nalla Neram (auspicious time) display

Android: the App asks only for approximate (coarse) location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). It does not request precise (GPS-level) or background location. You can change this at any time in Settings → Apps → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Permissions.

iOS: the App asks for location access while you are using it. You can choose not to share Precise Location, or change or remove access at any time, in Settings → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Location.

Location permission is optional. If you say no, you can choose a city yourself.

The App does not send your device location to us. It is used on your device only to work out the calculations above. Separately, the advertising, analytics and crash-reporting services in the App receive your IP address when they connect, and may use it to work out your approximate location (such as your city or region). That is why approximate location is listed in the table above.

📈 Analytics & Diagnostics

Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used, for example which screens are opened and which features are used. It uses a random app-instance identifier and may record device type, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, session information and in-app events. It also records purchase-related events (for example that a subscription or, on Android, a Jathagam slot was bought) so that we can see how paid features perform.

Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)

Crashlytics records app crashes and errors. A report contains a stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, the state of the device at the time, and a randomly generated installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs. It does not contain your name or email.

Firebase Remote Config

Remote Config lets us change app behaviour (for example switching a feature off) without an update. It uses the standard Firebase installation identifier.

📣 Advertising & Your Choices

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) to users who have not bought an ad-free plan. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA if you allow tracking), your IP address, device information (model, OS, language, time zone) and information about how you interact with ads (impressions and clicks). Depending on your choices, the ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

We currently use Google AdMob only. If we add another ad network in future, we will update this policy and the store privacy information before the change goes live.

Your advertising choices

  • iOS tracking permission: on iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before the IDFA can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent form: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options. This row only appears in regions where consent is required.
  • Android: to opt out of personalised ads, or to reset or delete your Advertising ID, go to Settings → Google → Ads on your device.
  • Remove ads entirely by buying an ad-free plan (see Purchases & Billing).

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

💳 Purchases & Billing

The App offers optional paid features: ad-free subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and a lifetime option. On Android, it also offers extra Jathagam profile slots and Jathagam plan benefits. The plans on offer, their prices and any free-trial offer are shown in the App and in the store before you buy.

Google Play Billing Android

On Android, all purchases are made through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or UPI details; Google handles them under Google Play's terms. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel: open the Google Play Store app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Cancel.

Apple App Store iOS

On iOS, all purchases are made with your Apple Account through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple handles them under its own terms. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged at confirmation of purchase. To cancel: open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Cancel Subscription.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to check and manage what you have purchased. RevenueCat receives the purchase information from Google Play or the App Store and an anonymous customer identifier created by its SDK. It does not receive your Google or Apple account name or your payment details. The anonymous identifier allows the App to restore your purchases after a reinstall or on a new device signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account, without a separate sign-in.

RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. We cannot process refunds ourselves.

  • Android: request a refund from your Google Play order history.
  • iOS: request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you need help, contact us and we will point you in the right direction.

🌐 Other Network Requests

To provide content, the App connects to some other services over HTTPS. Like any internet connection, these services can see your IP address and basic request details such as the type of device. We do not send them your name, your birth details or your profiles.

  • Google Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): the App reads festival and other content that we publish. It is read-only; the App does not write your data to it.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: used to display map images where the App shows a map for choosing a place.
  • Public rate services: used to fetch gold, silver and currency rates for the rates screen.

🤝 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not rent or trade it.

Data is shared only in these limited ways:

  • Service providers: as described above, identifiers, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics flow to Google (AdMob, Firebase), and purchase information flows to RevenueCat and to Google Play or Apple, so that the App can show ads, measure usage, prevent fraud and unlock paid features.
  • Advertising: data used for ads (such as the advertising identifier and approximate location) is shared with Google AdMob for advertising and measurement. Some privacy laws treat this as "sharing" for advertising. See Advertising & Your Choices to limit it.
  • Legal compliance: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.
  • Business transfer: if the App is acquired or merged, data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and we will tell users through an updated policy or an in-app notice.

🔒 Data Security

  • All network communication from the App uses HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Your settings, and on Android your Jathagam profiles, are stored locally in storage that is private to the App on your device.
  • Firebase, Google, Apple and RevenueCat apply their own security controls.
  • We do not store financial data. All payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

⚖️ Your Rights & Deleting Data

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: ask what information we hold that relates to you.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete data linked to your device or purchases.
  • Control of advertising: through the consent form, Settings → Privacy options, and your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android; Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iPhone).
  • Location: withdraw the location permission at any time in your device settings.

How to delete your data

  • Data kept on your device (settings, reminders, and on Android your Jathagam profiles) is removed when you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Device backups are controlled from your Google or Apple account settings.
  • To reset the advertising identifier, use Settings → Google → Ads on Android. On iPhone, turn off tracking for the App in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • To ask us to delete data held by our service providers (for example analytics or purchase records linked to your anonymous ID), email us at aara.apps.support@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Data that only Google, Apple or RevenueCat holds may have to be requested from them directly, and we will help you do that.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you can ask to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising identifiers shared with Google AdMob may count as "sharing" for cross-context advertising; you can limit this through your device ad or tracking settings and, where shown, Settings → Privacy options. To make a request, contact us using the details in Contact Us.

European Economic Area and UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where consent is required for advertising, we ask for it through Google's consent form and you can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Privacy options. We process crash and diagnostic data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

👶 Children's Privacy

Vetri Tamil Calendar is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, email aara.apps.support@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove it.
