Delete Your Data

Vetri Tamil Calendar

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

🗑️ Summary

Vetri Tamil Calendar has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Most of your data is on your own device and you can remove it yourself in a minute. A few things are held by Google and RevenueCat under random identifiers, and you can ask us to help delete those.

Fastest way: delete your Jathagam profiles in the App, then uninstall it. That removes everything the App stored on your device.

📱 1. Data on your device

Do any of these in the App, or all of them:

  • Jathagam and family profiles (names, birth date, time, place): open the Jathagam tab, open a profile and choose Delete.
  • Everything the App stored: Android Settings → Apps → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Storage → Clear data. This removes your profiles, bookmarks, reminders and settings. Or simply uninstall the App.
  • Location permission: Android Settings → Apps → Vetri Tamil Calendar → Permissions → Location → Don't allow.

Google backup: if Android backup was on, Google may keep a backup copy of the App's data under your Google account. You can remove it from your Google account's backup settings (Google Drive → Backups) or by turning backup off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

☁️ 2. Data held by services (not on your device)

The services built into the App use random identifiers, not your name. We hold no server database of our own about you. These records exist:

What Held by How to remove it
Analytics, diagnostics and crash data linked to a random app-installation ID Google (Firebase) Clearing the App's data or uninstalling starts a new random ID, so later activity is no longer linked to the old one. Email us to ask for deletion of earlier data.
Advertising ID and ad interaction data Google (AdMob) On your phone: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or reset it) and opt out of personalisation.
Purchase records and anonymous customer ID RevenueCat, Google Play Email us from the address you use with Google Play, and include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) so we can find the record. We will ask RevenueCat to delete it. Google Play keeps its own order history, which we cannot remove.

Deleting a purchase record does not cancel a subscription or give a refund. To cancel, open Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Note that deleting your purchase record may mean the App can no longer restore what you bought.

✉️ 3. Ask us to delete data

Send an email to the address below with the subject "Delete my data – Vetri Tamil Calendar" and tell us:

  • That you want your data deleted.
  • Your Google Play order number (GPA.…), if you made a purchase.
  • The approximate date you started using the App (this helps us check records).

We will confirm by email and complete the request within 30 days. We may keep limited information where the law requires it (for example, records of a paid purchase for tax or accounting).
